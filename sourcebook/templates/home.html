How a page is made

  1. Collect from open sources

    Places tagged on OpenStreetMap, plus a reviewed list of business websites.

  2. Read what each business publishes

    Its home, contact and about pages — respecting robots.txt — for products, phone, email, WhatsApp and Telegram.

  3. Describe from the facts only

    Short descriptions are written from those facts, with the source links kept beside them.

  4. Correct and export

    Anyone can suggest a correction; owners can ask for removal. Download any page as CSV or JSON.
